Whitford Merit Points

Your effort, rewarded

Merit Points are an ERC-20 token on Ethereum. They have no cash value and cannot be traded, so they always reward the student who earned them.

1

Earn

  • Full week of attendance +5
  • Assignment on time +10
  • Module passed +50
  • Peer tutoring, 4 weeks +30
  • Refer a friend +25
  • Graduation bonus +100
2

Spend on campus

  • Canteen coffee 30
  • Printing credit, EUR 5 50
  • Library late-fee waiver 40
  • Priority study room 20
  • Society or ball ticket 100
  • Give back: hardship fund 100
3

Rise through the tiers

  • Bronze up to 299 points: standard perks
  • Silver 300 to 799: extended library loans, early room booking
  • Gold 800+: priority career-fair appointments and the Dean's engagement list
