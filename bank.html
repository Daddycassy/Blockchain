How Skyline Bank uses Ethereum

The customer's money, interest and tax stay in the bank's core banking system. Only the certificate and the rewards live on-chain.

Customer opens FD→Bank verifies wallet (KYC)→FD certificate NFT issued→FD matures, NFT burned→Maturity Miles minted→Customer redeems a perk
  1. ERC-721 (SBOIFDCertificate): one unique, soulbound certificate per FD. It stores a reference, the term and the maturity date only.
  2. ERC-20 (SBOIRewardToken): Skyline Maturity Miles, 0 decimals, 1 token = Rs 1 of perks. Only KYC-verified wallets can hold them, and each mint is capped at 2,000.
  3. This website is the front end. It talks to the two contracts through MetaMask using the ethers.js library. Reads are free; writes are transactions you confirm.
  4. Safeguards: transfers of the certificate are blocked, early maturity is rejected, duplicate deposit references are rejected, and the bank can pause both contracts.
